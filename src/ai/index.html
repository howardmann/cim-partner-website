AI today · The foundation

AI that works the way engineers think.

The Agent's answers are only as good as what's underneath. Raw sensor feeds pass through a structuring transformer that classifies every point against one unified ontology. Once data is trusted, automation takes over.

Our AI Rules Engine encodes decades of mechanical and controls expertise into billions of automated real-time checks. It doesn't just detect problems — it triages them, prioritises what matters, and assigns actions directly to the right people.

Transparent, not a black box. Every rule, triage policy, and workflow automation can be tuned and explained. AI that works the way engineers think — at global scale.

Underneath · The data layer

Trusted data, classified by Warpspeed.

Building data is chaotic. Every system, every sensor, every vendor labels things differently. Warpspeed is our 109M-parameter language model, trained specifically for buildings on 20M+ sensors and ten years of operational data, classifying every point against one unified ontology.

It turns unstructured sensor feeds into clean, structured data. Every real-time point has meaning, context, and relationships — the foundation the Rules Engine above runs on.

Transparent, not a black box. Every prediction is validated and trained by engineers.

AI today · The foundation

Trusted data is the hard part. We solved it with Warpspeed.

Building data is chaotic. Every system, every sensor, every vendor labels things differently. Warpspeed is our 109M-parameter language model, trained specifically for buildings on 20M+ sensors and ten years of operational data, classifying every point against one unified ontology.

It turns unstructured sensor feeds into clean, structured data. Every real-time point has meaning, context, and relationships.

Transparent, not a black box. Every prediction is validated and trained by engineers. This is the foundation everything else on this page runs on.
